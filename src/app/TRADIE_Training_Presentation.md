## Slide 27: Stage 2 - Inspection
**[Visual: Inspection process flowchart]**

### Quality Verification:

**Inspection Process:**
1. Buyer requests inspection
2. Schedule mutually convenient time
3. Third-party or buyer inspects
4. Quality parameters checked:
   - Visual appearance
   - Moisture content
   - Impurities %
   - Grain size/uniformity
5. Report generated
6. Both parties review

**Outcomes:**
✅ Pass → Move to Sale  
❌ Fail → Renegotiate or cancel  
⚠️ Partial → Price adjustment  

*Speaker Notes: Explain that inspection protects both parties. Sellers prove quality, buyers verify before committing. Mention that third-party inspection costs are typically split.*
